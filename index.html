Useful examples: a weekly update, supplier reply, or meeting action list. A Gem does not send emails or update your records by itself.
STEP 14GEMINI GEMS

Create a Weekly Update Writer

Watch the demonstration, then follow the same steps.

  1. Open Gemini in your laptop browser. Open Gems / Explore Gems in the sidebar.
  2. Select New Gem. Name it Weekly Update Writer.
  3. Paste the text below into Instructions.
Saved instructions
ROLE: Help me write a weekly factory update for my manager.
TASK: Turn my rough notes into a clear update.
BACKGROUND: My manager needs a short report, especially issues needing a decision.
FORMAT: Use Done this week, Next week, and Need your decision. Use bullets and plain English; keep the update under 150 words. Write “None” when there is no decision needed.
CHECK: Do not invent numbers, names, dates, or promises. Put unclear information under Please check instead of guessing.

If Gems is not visible, check Settings and help. Available options depend on your company account.

STEP 15GEMINI GEMS

Test the Gem before saving

Paste these fictional notes into the preview chat.

Fictional notes for testing
Finished stock count for lines 2 and 3. Four cartons of gloves are missing; still checking.
Trained two new staff on packing.
Forklift battery is weak again. Replacement may cost 18,000 pesos; price not confirmed.
Mask supplier says delivery is next Friday; exact date not confirmed.
Next week: count line 4 and follow up on the missing gloves.

Check the format

Are the three report headings clear? Is it short and easy to read?

Check the facts

The missing gloves stay unresolved. The battery price and delivery date stay unconfirmed.

If something is wrong, edit Instructions and test again. Then click Save — previewing alone does not save the Gem.
